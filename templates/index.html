The Technology

Dilated Attention Network

Our model combines the power of DeepLabV3+ with a custom Channel and Spatial Attention Module (CBAM) to capture intricate details in high-resolution satellite imagery.

Global Context

Dilated convolutions allow the network to understand large-scale context without losing resolution, ensuring that both large structures and fine details are captured accurately.

Attention Mechanism

CBAM focuses on "what" is important (Channel Attention) and "where" it is (Spatial Attention), significantly improving the model's ability to distinguish between similar land cover types.
